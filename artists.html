THE ARTISTS

Meet the visionaries, revolutionaries, and master painters who redefined light, proportion, and human expression across centuries.

RENAISSANCE

Sandro Botticelli

Florentine (Italian) (1445 – 1510)

Alessandro di Mariano di Vanni Filipepi, known as Sandro Botticelli, was a premier master of the Florentine Early Renaissance. Patronized by the Medici family, his grace of line, poetical mythological allegories, and delicate emotional tone redefined 15th-century art.

"Art is the visible expression of divine poetry and linear rhythm."

KEY WORKS CATALOGUED

  • Water Lilies

  • Impression, Sunrise

  • Haystacks

  • Rouen Cathedral
